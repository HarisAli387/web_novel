The Story Behind
the Stories

Welcome to Novela by Haris — a sanctuary for modern Urdu literature and digital storytelling. What started as a personal passion for weaving words into worlds has evolved into this digital platform, sharing tales that resonate with the human heart.

I am Haris, the voice behind these stories. My journey as a writer began with a simple observation: that every life has a story worth telling, and every story has the power to change a life. Whether it's the eerie silence of a horror mystery or the gentle warmth of rural life, my aim is to transport you into the very soul of the narrative.

In this fast-paced world, I believe in the timeless magic of reading. Every novel here is crafted with meticulous care, ensuring that every word you read leaves an impression, a thought, or a feeling that lingers.

Thank you for joining me on this literary expedition. Your support is the fuel that keeps my pen moving. Dive in, explore, and let the stories take you where you've never been before.
